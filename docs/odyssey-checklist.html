Three that are not like the others read these twice

The ones with a body count behind them

Throttle-low arm interlock. Finding 5, and the specification says of it: “it is the one that hurts people”. Confirm arming is refused with the stick raised — not that it works with the stick down.

Motor direction, propellers removed. Props-out, and each diagonal pair must match: M1 with M4, M2 with M3. Get this wrong with propellers fitted and the aircraft inverts itself into the ground at full throttle.

IMU polarity. Nose-down must give positive pitch, right-wing-down positive roll. An inversion here diverges on the first take-off, and there is no recovering from it in the air.

And one procedure that was replaced because it could not work

The revision 1.0 free-fall test — drop the craft 10 cm, or invert it — was arithmetically incapable of passing. A 10 cm drop gives 143 ms of free fall against a 400 ms threshold, and inverting leaves the accelerometer magnitude at 9.8 m/s², nowhere near the 1.5 m/s² trigger. It would have “passed” by never firing. Use the tethered drop rig from §11.3 instead.
