Eighteen Found By Reading

A review of revision 1.0 found eighteen defects, seven of them critical. Fixing them surfaced forty-six more — and several were worse than anything in the original list. This is the account of how a specification that looked finished turned out not to be.

The shape of it 18 by review · 46 by doing

The review was the cheapest pass, and it found the smallest share

Eighteen defects came from reading the document carefully. Forty-six came from trying to build what it described — compiling it, running it on a board, costing its own procedures, and drawing its diagrams.

That ratio is the argument for every check in this repository. Reading finds what contradicts itself on the page. Only doing finds what contradicts reality.

#What it wasFound by

Three of these were caused by fixing an earlier one

Finding 43 came from finding 41's fix: moving a pin off the USB data lines put it on GPIO 39, which the low-power UART cannot reach. Finding 44 came from 43's fix: the replacement port was already the GNSS's. And the blackbox gate ordering was found while fixing the volatile defect one layer below it.

A fix is a change, and a change is a chance to be wrong. That is not an argument against fixing things — it is the argument for a check per fix.

What each defect left behind

The useful output of a finding is not the fix. It is the check that makes the whole class of defect unable to recur — and most of these exist because something got through once.
